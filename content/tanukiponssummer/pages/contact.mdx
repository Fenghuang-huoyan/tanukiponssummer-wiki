Use the channels below depending on whether your question is about **this website (`tanukiponssummer.wiki`)** or **the video game itself**.

## 1. Contact this website (Editorial, Privacy & Copyright)

If you spot an outdated patch note, an achievement description change, a mobile layout issue on our [Completion Checklist](/checklist), or have a copyright/attribution or [Privacy Policy](/privacy) inquiry regarding **tanukiponssummer.wiki**:

- **Site Editorial & Compliance Email:** `editorial@tanukiponssummer.wiki`
- **What to include:** The page URL on `tanukiponssummer.wiki`, what needs updating, and (for factual game corrections) a link to or screenshot of the official patch note or in-game screen.

We review factual correction notes regularly and update the page's verification timestamp once confirmed.

## 2. Official game support (Denkiworks & CRITICAL REFLEX)

<Callout kind="note" title="Important: We are not the game developer">
**tanukiponssummer.wiki** is an unofficial fan guide. We have no access to Steam accounts, game code, or console patches.
</Callout>

If you need technical support for **TANUKI: Pon's Summer** (crashes, controller bindings, save files, or gameplay bugs):

- **Official Publisher Support Email (listed on Steam):** `support@criticalreflex.com`
- **Official Bug Report Form (from 10 Nov 2026 Steam launch post):** `https://critical-reflex.fillout.com/tanuki-bug-report` (also accessible via the in-game title-screen button added in the 11 November patch).
- **Official Community Discord:** [CRITICAL REFLEX Discord](https://discord.gg/v2m7Ju7YM)
