Pon’s job, in the store’s short description, is part-time work at the Post Office. The long description is the whole routing guide Steam printed: ride the BMX, carry letters and packages, fit the awkward boxes, do not keep people waiting, and learn the routes until the island feels like home.

## What “delivery routes” means in the store text

Two official texts use the word routes. Neither draws them.

The Steam “about” paragraph says you learn your routes and get to know the people along them. The 10 November 2026 press release is the longer version: “Get to know your delivery routes. Hop on your BMX and explore the island at your leisure, discovering new routes, ramps, and rails. Learning your way around will help you plan your journeys and maybe even uncover some surprises.”

“Maybe even uncover some surprises” is the press line. It does not say what the surprise is. Ramps and rails in that sentence are explored on the [BMX](/bmx) page. They are not given coordinates here.

The 4 November post is a day-in-the-life sketch. It starts at the Post Office, with stacked parcels and “friendly postal workers,” then moves on to a bar, insects, sumo, and baseball. Those later stops are invitations, not mail stops. They are summarized on the [guide](/guide).

## Packages on the bike

The store’s warning is about shape, not about a weight stat. “Plan your delivery to fit all the awkwardly-shaped boxes on a bike.” The press release says the same thing in plainer words: figure out how to pack letters and parcels of every shape and size so they arrive safely.

The banner on this page is a store screenshot of a tall stack of boxes over the front basket. Another frame, above, shows a single box in that basket. The screenshots illustrate the “awkwardly shaped” line. They are not a packing diagram, and the UI in them was not readable as a route name on the copies saved here.

A third frame shows a dock, a boat, a crane, and three packages in the water.

![A fishing boat and a crane sit beside a wooden dock. Three packages float in the water, and a bridge crosses the cliff behind them.](/images/official/steam/ss-05.jpg)

The 28 October 2026 announcement says, “Maybe someone will even let Pon operate a crane?” The word is maybe. The screenshot has no caption on the store page that turns the crane into a delivery contract. This page does not add one.

## The post office, as the sources show it

The short description calls the job part-time. The press release’s joke is that you are “the newest (and possibly only) mail-delivery tanuki” on the island. “Possibly only” is the press office’s wording. It is not a roster.

A store screenshot of the counter shows Pon from behind, a clerk in a postal cap behind the desk, and a coworker in a blue dress. No name is printed on that frame. This page does not invent one. Sakamori, the name on a different screenshot, is at the bar in the 4 November post, not behind this counter. He is mentioned on the [guide](/guide).

The 11 November 2026 patch does not list a route change. Its only place line is a ramp in the main town, which belongs with the bike notes.
