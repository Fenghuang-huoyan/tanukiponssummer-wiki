Effective date: **23 November 2026**. This Privacy Policy explains what information is processed when you visit **tanukiponssummer.wiki** ("this site").

## 1. No user accounts or personal form collection

This site is built and served as a static reference website. We do **not** offer user registration, login accounts, newsletters, or public comment boards, and we never ask for your Steam credentials, save files, or payment details.

## 2. Local browser storage (`localStorage`)

When you use interactive tools such as the [Completion Checklist](/checklist), your checked milestones are saved exclusively in your own web browser under the `localStorage` key `tanuki_pons_summer_checklist_v1`. That data remains on your device, is never transmitted to our servers, and can be cleared at any time using the "Reset all" button on the tool or your browser's site-data settings.

## 3. Analytics (Google Analytics 4)

Content pages load **Google Analytics 4** (`G-JM7HW2NZLJ`) to understand aggregated traffic patterns such as visited pages, approximate country/region, and device viewport categories. Google Analytics uses first-party cookies to distinguish unique sessions without identifying you personally to us. You can opt out via browser privacy settings or the official [Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).

## 4. Advertising & Google AdSense cookies

To keep our guides and interactive tools free to use, this site may display advertisements served by **Google AdSense** (once enabled in site configuration).

- Third-party vendors, including **Google**, use cookies (such as the DoubleClick DART cookie) to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites on the internet.
- Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet.
- Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting [Google Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com/) or [aboutads.info](https://www.aboutads.info/choices/).
- Details on how Google processes data on partner sites are published at [policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## 5. Outbound store & affiliate links

Pages on this site link to official storefronts (such as the Steam Store) and may display offers from authorized PC digital key retailers (such as Fanatical, Humble Store, or Green Man Gaming). If you click a `rel="sponsored"` partner link and complete a purchase on that third-party storefront, we may earn a small referral commission at no additional cost to you. Any purchase you make is governed solely by that storefront's own privacy policy and terms.

## 6. Your privacy rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Visitors in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, and California may exercise rights regarding cookie consent, ad personalization, and data access through browser cookie controls and the Google privacy links listed above. Because we store zero personal profiles on our servers, we hold no personal account records to sell or export.

## 7. Contact

For questions regarding this Privacy Policy or site data practices, visit the [Contact page](/contact) or write to `editorial@tanukiponssummer.wiki`.
