The store’s trick paragraph is short. The little BMX can do more than carry the post. You launch off ramps, catch a rail, and string tricks together on the island’s paths and rooftops. Then it gives the only number the store page attaches to that work: over 100 BMX challenges.

## What the challenges are said to be

The 10 November 2026 press release repeats the store almost line for line: launch off ramps, catch a rail, string tricks together, over 100 challenges. The delivery section of that same release treats ramps and rails as things you find while learning routes. Riding them is part of getting around, not a separate mode with its own menu description.

The 10 November launch post is looser and more specific at the same time. It calls Pon the local BMX nut, calls every road a runway, every roof gap an invitation, and every rail a challenge, and it tells you to warm up bunny hops. “100 and 1 BMX challenges await” is the count in that post. The 28 October release-date post uses the same “100 and 1.”

Bunny hops, roof gaps, and “shred the streets” are color in the announcement. They are not a trick list with scores. The store never prints a point value, a medal, or a fail condition.

The banner is a store frame of a grind on a yellow rail above the roofs. The frame above is a wheelie beside a pagoda. Both match the “rails, paths, and rooftops” sentence. Neither frame has a challenge title in the image this site saved.

## A ramp the patch mentions

On 11 November 2026 the patch notes for `1.0.2.ccc94e3aa6x00` say the team “adjusted ramp in main town to prevent player from getting stuck.”

“Main town” is the only place name in that note. The note does not say which ramp, what the old collision was, or whether the change moved a challenge. It does tell you that a ramp in the main town existed at launch and was adjusted the next day. The other two bullets are a title-screen bug-report button and a shrine decoration, covered on the [shrine](/shrine) page.

The press release’s advice for routes is the practical line that is actually printed: learning your way around, including the ramps and rails, helps you plan a delivery. How a stuck ramp would have changed a route is not something this site can say, because the note does not describe the spot. Mail stops, such as they were printed, are on [Delivery](/delivery).

## The bike and the pad

Steam sets `controller_support` to full. The category list includes Full controller support, Gamepad Recommended, DualShock controller support, and DualSense controller support. The store still describes the BMX in keyboard-and-mouse-neutral verbs: launch, catch a rail, string tricks. It does not print a button map.

This site has not played the challenges and has not tested a pad. The recommendation is the category label, not a measured result.

The launch announcement invites play “on Steam and Deck” in the same sentence as the console storefronts. The appdetails `platforms` field for app 3218570 is Windows, with Mac and Linux false. A Steam Deck compatibility badge was not a field in the payload saved for this page, so none is claimed here.
