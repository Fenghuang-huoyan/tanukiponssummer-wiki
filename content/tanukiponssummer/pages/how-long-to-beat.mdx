When players search **how long to beat TANUKI: Pon's Summer**, they usually want two answers: how the **one-month festival deadline** actually ticks in-game, and how big the gap is between rolling credits at the festival versus finishing **100% of the island's side content**. Because Denkiworks and CRITICAL REFLEX do not print an official hour estimate on the Steam store page, exact hour averages remain **Unverified / ❓**—but public developer interviews, shipped-build reviews, and Steam's 43 global achievement rates show the structure clearly.

## 1. In-game length: one summer month to the Matsuri

Every official summary anchors the campaign to **one summer month**:

- **The deadline** — The Steam short description states: *"The Tanuki Festival is just a month away, and the shrine is in disrepair."* Reaching the finale unlocks *The Great Tanuki Matsuri* (*"Host the Great Tanuki Matsuri!"*) (**Verified / ✔**). Whether that month equals 28, 30, or 31 playable shifts—or skips certain calendar dates—is **Unverified / ❓** in store copy.
- **How a single day advances** — In a Tokyo Game Show interview published by [Game\*Spark on 23 September 2026](https://en.gamespark.jp/article/2026/09/23/310.html), Ari of Denkiworks explained that activities exist even if you skip deliveries and that **you will not run out of time in real time** while doing them (**Verified / ✔** quote from pre-release interview). Shipped-game reviews align with that pacing: [VGC (9 Nov 2026)](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/review/tanuki-pons-summer-review/) describes each in-game day functioning like a chapter where you finish that day's missions and end the day, and [Games Asylum (17 Nov 2026)](https://www.gamesasylum.com/2026/11/17/tanuki-pons-summer-review/) notes the shrine spirit sends Pon home once daily deliveries and refurbishments are complete. Exact post-game free-roam rules after the festival are **Unverified / ❓**.

## 3. Does demo progress shorten the full game?

If you played the Steam demo before launch, official announcements confirm save carry-over (**Verified / ✔**), though two slightly different descriptions were printed:

- **26 August & 28 October 2026 announcements**: stated that progress from the **first three days** of the demo carries over to the full release.
- **10 November 2026 launch announcement**: advised players not to delete demo saves because *"the progress will be transferred to the main game automatically."*

How much real-world time those transferred opening days save depends on your demo file and remains **Unverified / ❓** on this wiki.
