The shrine is the month's job. The store opens on a building in disrepair and a festival a month away. Steam's achievement text names the repairs and the festival you host at the end. It does not name a price. Where a cost, a material count, or a required sequence is missing from the Steam text and the official posts, this page marks it **未核实**.

## Do these in the client

Work down this list. Steps 1 to 4 are the opening the achievement descriptions name, and on 18 November 2026 the global unlock rates fell in the same direction. From the chozuya onward, restore whatever the shrine is offering. The required sequence among those later pieces is 未核实.

1. **Finish the opening tutorial.** The achievement Summer Starts reads "Pon's summer begins! Finish the opening tutorial."
2. **Finish the first day at the Post Office.** Postal Probation reads "Complete your first day of work at the Post Office." The store and the 10 November press release say the money and rewards from that work are what you put into the shrine. A day's pay is 未核实. The route itself stays on [Delivery](/delivery).
3. **Finish the Shrine Building tutorial.** Building Blocks reads "Complete the Shrine Building tutorial." Which object that lesson places, and what it costs, is 未核实. It is the game's own shrine lesson, and 31.7% of Steam players had it on 18 November, against 16.5% for the torii.
4. **Restore the torii gate when the shrine offers it.** Torii Troubles reads "Restore the Torii Gate!" The 10 November launch post also says that on the festival day the torii gates should not fall on anyone.
5. **Restore the chozuya, the komainu statues, and the ema stand as each one is offered.** The three achievement lines are "Restore the Chozuya!", "Restore the Komainu Statues!", and "Restore the Ema Stand!" No source checked here prints a price or a gate that forces one of them to wait on another.
6. **Place the decorations you want visitors to see, including benches and lamps.** Purchases are tracked apart from the four repairs. The festival-day check from the launch post is below.
7. **Host the festival.** The Great Tanuki Matsuri reads "Host the Great Tanuki Matsuri!" The store says that is when you welcome the residents you spent the month meeting.

The jobs that earn the money and the rewards are listed on the [guide](/guide). The 13 November announcement says the in-game shop takes coins, including for hats, and tells players who spend them to work a few extra jobs because the shrine still has to be restored. A coin price for any shrine piece is 未核实.

The rates do not split those two into a sequence. A required build order is 未核实.

Ari of Denkiworks, interviewed by Game\*Spark at Tokyo Game Show and published 23 September 2026, said the restoration has stages and that you gradually unlock major objects such as the torii, the water ablution pavilion, ema plaques, and komainu as Pon helps residents. The build was described then as 95% done. That sentence lists ema before komainu. The shipped achievement schema lists komainu before ema. This page does not pick a winner from a pre-release interview and a name list. In the client, take the repair that is actually offered.

The same interview says activities exist for players who skip deliveries, and that you will not run out of time in real time by doing them. A Video Games Chronicle review dated 9 November 2026, of a PC build, describes each in-game day as a chapter: finish that day's missions, then end the day. A Games Asylum review dated 17 November 2026 says the key shrine events play out in a linear way and that the shrine's spirit sends Pon home once the day's deliveries and refurbishments are done. The exact button the current client uses to end a day is 未核实. What you can do with those accounts is finish the repair the day is offering, then sleep when the game asks. No official page prints a minute timer.

## Decorations you can buy

Two achievements count purchases and nothing else: Shrine Spender, "Purchase over 50 Shrine Decorations," and Dedicated Decorator, "Purchase over 100 Shrine Decorations." A catalog and a price for any one object are 未核实.

The 11 November 2026 patch, build `1.0.2.ccc94e3aa6x00`, has one shrine line: "Fixed issue with parking meter shrine decoration becoming unselectable after placement." That confirms a decoration named parking meter, and it confirms that decorations are placed. It does not say where the parking meter is bought, what it costs, or that the festival requires one. The other two patch lines are a ramp in the main town and a bug-report button on the title screen. The ramp is on the [BMX](/bmx) page. Steam's news list on 18 November 2026 had no shrine patch after that one. The 13 November post is a sale, a hat shop, trading cards, and a trailer.
