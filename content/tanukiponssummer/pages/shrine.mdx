The shrine is the month's job. The store opens on a building in disrepair and a festival a month away. Steam's achievement text names the repairs and the festival you host at the end. It does not name a price. Where a cost, a material count, or a required sequence is missing from the Steam text and the official posts, this page marks it **Unverified / ❓**.

## Festival preparation and shrine restoration milestones

Progression through the summer festival follows the achievement structure published on Steam. Steps 1 through 4 align with early progression and global unlock statistics. Beyond early milestones, players address repairs as presented in-game; strict sequential requirements and material costs remain **Unverified / ❓** against public documentation.

1. **Finish the opening tutorial** — **Verified / ✔** (Steam achievement: *Summer Starts* — "Pon's summer begins! Finish the opening tutorial.").
2. **Finish the first day at the Post Office** — **Verified / ✔** (Steam achievement: *Postal Probation* — "Complete your first day of work at the Post Office."). The store and the 10 November 2026 press release confirm earnings and rewards from postal work fund shrine restoration. Specific daily earnings figures are **Unverified / ❓**. Delivery routes are detailed on [Delivery](/delivery).
3. **Finish the Shrine Building tutorial** — **Verified / ✔** (Steam achievement: *Building Blocks* — "Complete the Shrine Building tutorial."). Which initial object this tutorial constructs and associated material costs are **Unverified / ❓**. (Steam global rate: 31.7% on 18 Nov 2026, versus 16.5% for the torii).
4. **Restore the Torii Gate** — **Verified / ✔** (Steam achievement: *Torii Troubles* — "Restore the Torii Gate!"; 10 November launch announcement notes keeping torii gates standing). Specific coin prices, materials, and mandatory build order are **Unverified / ❓**.
5. **Restore the Chozuya, Komainu Statues, and Ema Stand** — **Verified / ✔** (Steam achievements: *Chozuya* — "Restore the Chozuya!", *Komainu Statues* — "Restore the Komainu Statues!", *Ema Stand* — "Restore the Ema Stand!"). No public documentation establishes an enforced sequential order or material recipes among these three (**Unverified / ❓**).
6. **Place shrine decorations (benches and lamps)** — **Verified / ✔** (Steam achievements *Shrine Spender* [>50 decorations] and *Dedicated Decorator* [>100 decorations]; launch post highlights benches and lamps). Specific threshold numbers for the festival day are **Unverified / ❓**.
7. **Host the Great Tanuki Matsuri** — **Verified / ✔** (Steam achievement: *The Great Tanuki Matsuri* — "Host the Great Tanuki Matsuri!"). Store text confirms welcoming island residents met throughout the month.

The jobs that earn money and rewards are detailed on the [guide](/guide). The 13 November announcement notes the in-game shop accepts coins for items like hats and reminds players to reserve funds for shrine repairs. Specific coin costs for shrine restoration pieces remain **Unverified / ❓**.

The build was described then as 95% done. That sentence lists ema before komainu. The shipped achievement schema lists komainu before ema. This page does not pick a winner from a pre-release interview and a name list. In the client, take the repair that is actually offered.

The exact interaction or menu button the client uses to end a day is **Unverified / ❓**. What players can do with those accounts is finish the repair the day offers, then sleep when prompted. No official page prints a minute timer.

## Decorations you can buy

Two achievements count purchases and nothing else: Shrine Spender, "Purchase over 50 Shrine Decorations," and Dedicated Decorator, "Purchase over 100 Shrine Decorations." A catalog and individual coin prices for objects are **Unverified / ❓**.

The 11 November 2026 patch, build `1.0.2.ccc94e3aa6x00`, has one shrine line: "Fixed issue with parking meter shrine decoration becoming unselectable after placement." That confirms a decoration named parking meter, and it confirms that decorations are placed. It does not say where the parking meter is bought, what it costs, or that the festival requires one. The other two patch lines are a ramp in the main town and a bug-report button on the title screen. The ramp is on the [BMX](/bmx) page. Steam's news list on 18 November 2026 had no shrine patch after that one. The 13 November post is a sale, a hat shop, trading cards, and a trailer.
