## Track-by-track summary & bottleneck notes

### 1. Story & Great Tanuki Shrine repairs (8)

These eight achievements trace Pon's main objective from day one to festival day: *Summer Starts* (`79.6%`), *Postal Probation* (`61.2%`), *Building Blocks* (`33.3%`), *Torii Troubles* (`18.8%`), *Chozuya Champion* (`6.2%`), *Komainu Kudos* (`4.5%`), *Ema Ending* (`4.5%`), and *The Great Tanuki Matsuri* (`3.9%`). Exact coin costs, resource counts, and whether the later three structures must be built in a fixed order are **Unverified / ❓**; see [Shrine Upgrades](/shrine).

### 2. Shrine decoration milestones (2)

*Shrine Spender* requires purchasing **over 50** shrine decorations (`11.8%`), and *Dedicated Decorator* requires **over 100** (`4.5%`). Notice that more Steam players hit 50 decorations (`11.8%`) than restore the Chozuya (`6.2%`), showing players buy decorations steadily throughout the month rather than waiting until the end. Individual shop prices remain **Unverified / ❓**.

### 3. Resident delivery jobs (14)

Fourteen achievements follow the exact pattern *"Deliver to [Resident] and [complete activity]"*. Two of these directly introduce collection mechanics:

- **Final Summer (`26.7%`)** — *"Deliver to Musashi and learn to catch Bugs."*
- **Frustrated Fishing (`20.7%`)** — *"Deliver to Ryota and learn how to Fish."*

On the screenshot banner at the top of this page, Pon carries a flat parcel into an arcade with claw machines, matching **Box Clawing (`27.0%`)** (*"Deliver to Kacho and install the new Machines"*). Pay rates, mini-game target scores, and whether any resident job is missable before the festival are **Unverified / ❓**; see the full resident breakdown on the [Walkthrough](/guide).

![Official Steam screenshot showing Pon inside an arcade with claw machines while carrying a parcel.](/images/official/steam/ss-10.jpg)

### 4. BMX challenges & trick score records (9)

Nine achievements cover bike mastery:

- **Challenge clears (4)**: *Amateur Rider* (10 challenges, `18.4%`), *Semi-pro Rider* (25, `9.9%`), *Expert Rider* (50, `5.0%`), and *Undisputed BMX World Champion!* (all challenges, `1.1%`). Official copy calls the total "over 100" on the store page and "100 and 1" in two announcements; see [BMX](/bmx).
- **Single-combo records (4)**: *25K-Combo!* (`8.2%`), *50K-Combo!* (`4.6%`), *75K-Combo!* (`3.5%`), and *One Hundred Thousand Combo?!* (`3.1%`).
- **Single-day cumulative score (1)**: *Daily Millionaire* — *"Clear over 1,000,000 points total in a day"* (`2.3%`). Best grinding spots and combo multipliers are **Unverified / ❓**.

### 5. Island Fishing & Island Insects (10)

The rarest unlocks on Steam (`0.5%` to `1.6%` on 23 November 2026) belong to the two five-tier encyclopedias:

- **Fishing (5)**: *Chum Catcher* (Common, `1.0%`), *Rare Reel* (Rare, `0.5%`), *Estuary Epics* (Epic, `0.6%`), *Lure Legends* (Legendary, `1.6%`), and *King Angler* (all island fish, `0.5%`). Note that Steam's original English description spells *King Angler* as *"Succesfully catch all of the island's Fish!"* (one *s* in *Succesfully*).
- **Insects (5)**: *Cheap Catch* (Common, `0.7%`), *Repellent Rares* (Rare, `0.5%`), *Earthy Epics* (Epic, `0.6%`), *Legendary Larva* (Legendary, `0.5%`), and *Net King* (all island insects, `0.5%`).

Species lists, spawn times, weather conditions, and total species counts are not printed in public Steam documentation and remain **Unverified / ❓**.
