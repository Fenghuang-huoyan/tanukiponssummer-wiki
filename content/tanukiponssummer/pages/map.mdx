Players searching for a **TANUKI: Pon's Summer map** run into the same design choice printed on the Steam store page: the official copy tells you to *"hop on your BMX and explore the island at your leisure, discovering new routes, ramps, and rails"* rather than handing out a published street grid. Whether the shipped client includes a pause-menu map, compass, or waypoint marker is **Unverified / ❓** from public store files; this page compiles every **named landmark, resident venue, and visual district clue** that official sources actually confirm (**Verified / ✔**).

## Real-world inspirations vs. the fictional island

Three official sources define what the setting is—and what it is not:

1. **A fictional Japanese island** — **Verified / ✔** (28 October 2026 Steam release-date announcement).
2. **Shaped by four real Japanese cities: Kyoto, Aomori, Sapporo, and Beppu** — **Verified / ✔** (official [CRITICAL REFLEX game page](https://www.criticalreflex.com/tanuki), which notes the towns reflect the developers' lived experiences in those four cities; the 10 November press release is also datelined Kyoto, home of debut studio Denkiworks).
3. **Are those four real city names used as literal in-game town names?** — **Unverified / ❓**. Official store copy says *"distinct towns"* in one line and *"the island"* throughout; it does not publish a district roster or map legend.

## How navigation connects to delivery & BMX

The 10 November 2026 press release ties island exploration directly to your daily shift:

- **Shortcuts double as trick lines** — Ramps, roof gaps, and yellow rails (`ss-09.jpg`) sit directly over tiled town roofs and beside coastal bridges, meaning BMX lines are built into the island's open roads rather than locked inside separate skatepark menus (**Verified / ✔**; see [BMX Challenges](/bmx)).
- **Delivery routes teach the layout** — Rather than giving turn-by-turn GPS text on the store page, the press release says learning your routes helps you plan how to carry awkwardly shaped boxes without keeping neighbors waiting (**Verified / ✔**; see [Delivery Routes](/delivery)).
