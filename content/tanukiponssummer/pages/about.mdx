This is a short English wiki for **TANUKI: Pon's Summer**, Steam AppID **3218570**, built as a static site for **tanukiponssummer.wiki**.

## Disclaimer

tanukiponssummer.wiki is an unofficial fan-made guide for *TANUKI: Pon's Summer*. It is **not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Denkiworks, CRITICAL REFLEX, or Valve**. All game content, names, artwork, and trademarks belong to their respective owners. The soundtrack listing on Steam, app 5530500, prints “© 2026 Denkiworks. Published by CRITICAL REFLEX.” This site does not sell the game, process purchases, or recover accounts.

Nothing here is account support. The base game’s store page lists support@criticalreflex.com. That mailbox belongs to the publisher, not to this wiki, and this wiki does not read it. Bug reports in the 10 November 2026 launch post go to the publisher’s form at `https://critical-reflex.fillout.com/tanuki-bug-report`. The 11 November patch says a title-screen button for bug reports was added. Use those, not this site.

The [guide](/guide), [delivery](/delivery), [shrine](/shrine), and [BMX](/bmx) pages quote dated Steam text and official posts. A route, an upgrade, or a challenge appears only as far as those sources described it. There is no codes page because those sources do not publish a redeem program.

<Callout kind="note" title="What “checked” means">
“Checked 12 Nov 2026” means the Steam API, the English store page, the publisher page, the press release, and the linked announcements were read that day. It does not mean someone finished the 10 November build or rode the challenges.
</Callout>

## Images

The library hero, the library header, the capsules, the store header, and the screenshots are official Steam store assets for AppID 3218570. They were downloaded from the Steam CDN and are served from this site so pages do not hotlink Steam. The file list and source URLs are in [ATTRIBUTION.txt](/images/official/steam/ATTRIBUTION.txt).

## Privacy

The site is static. It has no accounts and does not ask for your Steam login or your TANUKI: Pon's Summer save.

GA4 `G-JM7HW2NZLJ` is set in `analytics.gaId`. The base layout loads that tag on content pages.

The Adsterra Native Banner slot named `afterQuickAnswer` is enabled for tanukiponssummer.wiki. Zone id `f989d0f6f9b21ee2ee7c4c62b3e3d68d` loads from `https://bauval.org/21/f989d0f6f9b21ee2ee7c4c62b3e3d68d` on the homepage and guide pages. No advertising script from another game is included. Adult and sensitive categories stay off. The 404 page does not render a slot.

The same privacy note is in the footer of every page, under the heading Privacy. The disclaimer paragraph in that footer is the short form of this page.

## What this site will not invent

- A delivery map, a stop order, or pay per parcel.
- A shrine cost, a decoration catalog, or an upgrade order. The parking meter is named only because a patch fixed it.
- A numbered BMX challenge list. The store says “over 100.” Two announcements say “100 and 1.” Neither names them.
- A hat checklist beyond Hollow Knight: Silksong, PEAK, and Shovel Knight, which are the names the store prints.
- Redeem codes. None were in the sources checked on 12 November 2026.
