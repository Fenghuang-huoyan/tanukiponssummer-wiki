**TANUKI: Pon's Summer** has been out since 10 November 2026. The store gives Pon one month: deliver the mail, make friends, and restore a shrine before the festival. This page is the loop you can follow. The named repairs, the decoration counts, and the festival-day checks are on [Shrine](/shrine). Where a pay rate or a required order is missing from public documentation, the line is marked **Unverified / ❓**.

> The Tanuki Festival is just a month away, and the shrine is in disrepair. Follow Pon, a well meaning, if a bit lazy Tanuki with a part-time job at the Post Office, as he works to restore the shrine before summer ends. Deliver packages, make friends, and get the shrine ready for the big festival!

## Follow this loop

1. **Start the month** — **Verified / ✔**. The short description puts the festival a month away and the shrine in disrepair. The long description and the 10 November press release call it the Great Tanuki Festival. The launch post says Matsuri. Steam's hosting achievement is The Great Tanuki Matsuri. A calendar date inside the month is **Unverified / ❓**. The [shrine](/shrine) page keeps the names on one event.
2. **Work the Post Office** — **Verified / ✔**. Ride the BMX, fit the packages, and deliver them. The press release says learning the routes, the ramps, and the rails is how you plan the day. The [delivery](/delivery) page stops at what those sources actually print. A street map and per-parcel wages are **Unverified / ❓**.
3. **Take the job the resident offers after the delivery** — **Verified / ✔**. The store names sushi, a baseball game, and the sumo ring, then "what else your new friends have going on." The publisher page says friendship and loyalty open the invitations, and that the rewards are items as well as money. The 14 jobs Steam's achievement text names are in the table below. What each one pays is **Unverified / ❓**.
4. **Spend the earnings on the shrine before you empty the shop on hats** — **Verified / ✔**. The 13 November announcement says the in-game shop takes coins and tells you to spend them on hats, then work extra jobs because the shrine still has to be restored. Whether those coins are the same balance as the store's "money" and the publisher's "items" is **Unverified / ❓**. The repairs to buy first, and the pieces with no published price, are on [Shrine](/shrine).
5. **On the festival day, check the three things the launch post names** — **Verified / ✔**: enough benches, enough lamps, and torii gates that will not fall. The exact numerical counts are **Unverified / ❓**. Then host the festival. The achievement text says "Host the Great Tanuki Matsuri!"

The BMX challenges are a separate track. The store counts "over 100." The launch post says "100 and 1." No source says a trick score is a shrine material. The bike page is [BMX](/bmx).

The press release, datelined Kyoto, calls Denkiworks a Kyoto studio and this game its debut. It says the three co-founders previously worked together on Cursed to Golf. The publisher page says the island is based on the developers’ lived experience in Kyoto, Aomori, Sapporo, and Beppu. A 28 October 2026 announcement calls the setting a fictional Japanese island. This page reports all three lines. It does not turn a real city into a district in the game.

Genres on Steam are Casual, Indie, and Simulation. The publisher page’s short label is “Cute Life Sim x BMX Tricks.” The launch post calls Pon an “apple-sized tanuki.” That is a phrase in the announcement, not a measured height.

## Collectibles the sources name

The store, the press release, and the launch post describe crossover hats. The names they print are Hollow Knight: Silksong, PEAK, and Shovel Knight, plus “and more.” The 28 October announcement also says you can “cosplay your favourite bug with a mask.” The 13 November post says the in-game shop sells the hats for coins. No hat count and no hat price were on those pages. Both are **Unverified / ❓**.

Insects are the other collection the announcements describe. The 4 November post says Pon measures and catalogues each one, then releases it. A store screenshot shows him holding a large red beetle. The post does not name species or a total. Releasing them is part of the sentence, so this page does not call the beetle a permanent inventory item. Fishing and bug sets have their own achievements, including catching every fish and every insect. No official page says the festival waits on those sets. Treating them as festival requirements is **Unverified / ❓**.

## Demo saves

Three official posts talk about a demo, and they do not use the same limit.

- 26 August 2026: the save from the first three days of the demo will transfer to the full game.
- 28 October 2026: the first three days of the demo carry over. The post says to transfer that progress at release.
- 10 November 2026: do not delete demo saves. “The progress will be transferred to the main game automatically.”

The launch wording is wider than “first three days.” This site did not move a save, so it does not pick a winner. The base game’s `dlc` array on 18 November 2026 contained one id, soundtrack app **5530500**. A demo app id was not in that array. Whether the soundtrack is still marked coming soon in every region was not re-checked as a separate field this pass.

## Patches and the 13 November post

Steam’s announcement titled “Release (1.0.2.ccc94e3aa6x00) - Nov 11, 2026” has three bullets:

- Adjusted a ramp in the main town so the player does not get stuck.
- Added a button on the title screen for sending bug reports.
- Fixed a shrine decoration, the parking meter, that could not be selected after it was placed.

The launch post links a bug form at `https://critical-reflex.fillout.com/tanuki-bug-report`. That form is the publisher’s. The ramp is the only place-name in the patch. The parking meter is the only decoration the patch names. Both are repeated on the [BMX](/bmx) and [shrine](/shrine) pages.

The next announcement, “Special Correspondence: Bundle of News!” on 13 November 2026, does not add a shrine price. It announces a cinematic trailer, the hat shop, a discount through 19 November, bundles with other games, Steam Trading Cards, and a Live2D model of Pon. Steam’s news list on 18 November had nothing newer.

## No redeem codes

The store page, the publisher page, the press release, and the announcements checked on 18 November 2026 do not list a code to redeem. The soundtrack is a separate Steam app, not a code. This site does not have a codes page and does not invent one.
